# Personas

Personas are limited to the users and contexts supported by the current project materials.

## Supported Personas

### Electrician on an Active Construction Site

Context:

- Works in the field rather than at a desk.
- Needs conduit bend calculations during active construction work.
- May be using the app with dirt on the screen or heavy glare.

Needs:

- Quick-glance readability.
- High-contrast display.
- Fast dynamic calculations.
- Visual mapping from values to the physical conduit.

### Field User Needing Quick Bend Calculations

Context:

- Uses calculators for offsets, saddles, kicks, 90s, multiple bends, and reference data.
- Needs inputs and outputs connected to bend diagrams rather than disconnected form fields.

Needs:

- Visual-first SVG diagrams.
- No Submit or Calculate step.
- Persistent Bender, Material, and Trade Size context across views.

### User Working With Poor Connectivity

Context:

- May work in concrete basements or other low-connectivity areas.

Needs:

- Client-side logic.
- No backend dependency for core calculator use.
- Offline reliability in later PWA verification.
